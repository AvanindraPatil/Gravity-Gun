# Gravity Gun Neighbor

**The Half-Life 2 Gravity Gun in Hello Neighbor.** Pull, carry and launch almost anything in the house: furniture,
boxes, doors ripped off their hinges, even the Neighbor himself.

<!-- screenshot here -->

> Unofficial fan mod, not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve, tinyBuild or Dynamic Pixels.
> The mod contains nothing of Half-Life 2: the installer builds the gun from your own copy.

## Features

- **The Gravity Gun in your hand** (slot 1) in every level, with Half-Life 2's model, claws, glow, beam, animations and sounds.
- **Pull, hold, punt**: right mouse pulls / grabs / drops, left mouse launches. No weight limit; grab a door and it comes off its hinges.
- **Characters too**: lift the Neighbor and the mannequins, punt them across the room, knock them down with props.
- **Zero gravity** (Z / X), **push / turn / freeze** a held prop (mouse wheel, hold R, F).
- **Gravity jump**: left mouse at the floor launches you into the air.
- No invisible boundary walls, no "the Neighbor is near" tension effect.

## Requirements

- **Hello Neighbor** (the 2017 game) on Steam, Windows.
- **Half-Life 2** on Steam, installed (read once at install; it can be uninstalled afterwards).
- Nothing else: the installer brings [UE4SS](https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS) and a small Python it builds with.

## Install

1. Download `GravityGunNeighbor-1.0.0.zip` from Releases and unzip it anywhere (keep the folder until you uninstall).
2. Close Hello Neighbor and double-click **Install.bat** (under a minute; it finds both games through Steam).
3. Start Hello Neighbor and load a level: the gun is in your hand.

Keys and troubleshooting: `README.txt` in the zip. Uninstall: **Uninstall.bat**.
